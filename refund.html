Welcome to Markito. This Refund Policy explains how refunds are handled for payments made on our platform.

1. General Policy

Payments made for Product Listing, Premium Services, Featured Listings, Advertisements or any other paid services are generally non-refundable once the service has been activated.

2. Eligible Refund Cases

  • Duplicate payment for the same service.
  • Successful payment but service was not activated due to a technical issue.
  • Payment deducted but transaction failed.
  • Any other refund approved by Markito after verification.

3. Non-Refundable Cases

  • Seller changes their mind after purchasing.
  • Product listing has already been published.
  • Violation of Markito Terms & Conditions.
  • Account suspended due to policy violations.

4. Refund Request

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days from the payment date with valid payment proof.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 7–14 business days. The actual credit time depends on your bank or payment provider.

6. Contact Support

For refund-related questions, please contact the official Markito Support Team through the support options available inside the Markito platform.
